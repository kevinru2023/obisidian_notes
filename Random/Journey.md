When looking into movies that did not follow the hero's journey I had one movie pop in my head, Pulp Fiction by Quentin Tarantino. Pulp Fiction is a movie that follows many different characters, and many different storylines. One unique aspect of Pulp Fiction is protagonist that makes a crazy self transformation or a protagonist that embarks on a grand adventure. Instead it takes multiple characters and showcases each one dealing with their own dilemmas. The movie itself doesn't really have a central story, instead it focuses on these different characters and their various stories such as Vincent grappling with moral issues despite being a gangster or the story of Marsellus Wallace and his wife Mia. Tarantino makes this movie work by abandoning the typical Hero's Journey and instead intertwines these complex stories to make an all time classic movie that many have considered one of the greats.
