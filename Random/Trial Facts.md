# Question 1:  Is an Instagram AI an Interactive Computer Service as defined in section 230?

This one is a bit difficult as under strict definition, AI is not human and therefore as section 230, "information provided by another information content provider" would not apply as a content provider in the context of section 230 would be another human. However, in the case of AI, this definition could not apply. 

---
# Question 2: Should there be a legal distinction between content produced by users and the algorithms designed by Meta to focus attention?

Yes in fact there already sort of is, in section 230 clearly outlined in the definition of an information content provider is : "means any person or entity that is responsible, in whole or in part, for the creation or development of information provided through the Internet or any other interactive computer service". So if we are talking legally here, it would be important for Meta or other entities make users aware of what content is produced by AI and what content is produced by users.
