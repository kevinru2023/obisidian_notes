**My Mandate: Alternative** 

**How does somebody in my Mandate think?:**
I think somebody in my mandate, tries their hardest to be different from the normal, they take stands that might not seem as popular and often standout apart from others. However, because of this quality they also are sometimes the most accepting people and don't judge others harshly because of their differences, it's also this same fact that makes these people really aggressive to those who do judge others because of their differences at times.  

**How would this Mandate run instagram?:**
If I were apart of this Mandate and I was running instagram, the first thing I would probably do is to try and censor the discriminatory posts that target select groups whether that be groups of certain races, religions, or sex. I think this mandate is mostly against bringing down others while also maintaining individual freedom, so apart from the initial censorship of hateful content, I think this mandate would actually try to limit censorship other than hateful content and push free speech.
