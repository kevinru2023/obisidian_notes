# Car brothers

This show would be similar to pimp my ride and property brothers. 

The show would consist of two brothers who go around different places and grab the shittiest cars they can find and make them brand new. I think it would be entertaining to watch two brothers go around fix peoples cars for them and make them brand new. I think most people enjoy watching people fix stuff and make it like brand new just because I think this generation really appreciates things like that, plus it would bring back the vibes that property brothers brought.

This could defiantly be something that people would watch on youtube since it's something that can be shown quick.
