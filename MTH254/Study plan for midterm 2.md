# Notes: 

- The gradient of a function is defined to be a vector field
	- The gradient is **a vector operation** that operates on a scalar function to produce a vector whose magnitude is the maximum rate of change of the function at the point of the gradient and which is pointed in the direction of that maximum rate of change
